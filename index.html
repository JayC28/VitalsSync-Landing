VITALSYNC HEALTH OS

The product layer can run within a broader health operating system.

Base44 provides the functioning consumer-facing platform. GitHub is the engineering source of truth for the evolving Health OS, which is being built as a modular platform capable of connecting consumer health, clinical workflows and interoperability.

Clinical intelligence

AI-assisted assessment, patient timelines, risk detection, monitoring and predictive deterioration architecture.

Workflow orchestration

Alert repositories, escalation pathways, provider routing and automated dispatch workflows designed to help prioritize care activity.

Interoperability & security

FHIR resources, LOINC-based observations, RBAC, audit logging, SMART on FHIR validation, data masking and a CI/CD-oriented engineering path.

Development status: interoperability and security validation, predictive-risk architecture and production hardening are ongoing. rPPG remains under calibration and validation.
